# Concept: Sourcing Signals

## Why

People who want to buy responsibly often see simple supplier ratings without enough context to tell what was measured, who supplied the information, or what is still unknown. A single score can hide gaps and imply more certainty than the evidence supports.

## For whom

Curious shoppers, students, and small teams learning how to ask better questions about ethical sourcing.

## What

Sourcing Signals is a small, static, interactive educational demo. Visitors can explore clearly fictional supplier profiles, inspect evidence-status labels across sourcing topics, and filter for profiles with a particular kind of disclosure.

## Intended outcome

After using the demo, a visitor should understand that responsible sourcing research involves multiple topics, evidence quality matters, and missing information is not proof of good or bad practice.

## Boundaries

- All supplier names, profile details, and examples are invented for demonstration.
- The demo will make no claims about real companies, workers, places, or certifications.
- It will not rank suppliers, calculate an ethics score, or recommend a purchase.
- It will not collect personal information or send data to a server.
- This is an educational prototype, not an audit or sourcing decision tool.
